The heavens and the hollow places

2 Enoch 3–22 (longer recension); 1 Enoch 22

Ten real heavens rise above the ground (2 Enoch's own First through Tenth — Muzaloth, Kuchavim, and Aravoth are named only in the longer Slavonic recension) and four real hollow places hold the dead beneath it (1 Enoch 22). Both are drawn as real belt-rings in the 3D dome and as the two clickable ladders flanking the disc above. Disclosed correction: an earlier framing of this said "three heavens and three hells" — that figure isn't in 1, 2, or 3 Enoch. The real, accurate count from the books themselves is ten and four.

In the 3D view, the sun, moon, and sky-portals now sit at the real height of the Fourth heaven — 2 Enoch's own "the courses of sun and moon" — instead of hovering just over the ground. The real zodiac (Eighth heaven, Muzaloth) and the real fixed stars (Ninth heaven, Kuchavim) are drawn at their own real heights too, each with its own clickable marker.
